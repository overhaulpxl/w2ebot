# Way 2 Eternal (W2E) Bot Privacy Policy

## What Data We Collect
When you interact with the W2E Bot, we collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide our RPG, Economy, and Middleman services:
- **Discord ID & Username**: Used to create your RPG profile, track your economy balance (ETM/ECY), and log your middleman deals.
- **Message Content**: Used exclusively when you trigger bot commands (via `w!` prefix) or interact with the Gemini AI persona. We temporarily store recent chat history solely to provide conversational context for the AI.
- **Server Activity**: We track message timestamps to award chat-based XP and calculate voice channel durations for minigame rewards.

## How We Use Your Data
- To manage your virtual economy wallet and RPG progression.
- To facilitate secure Middleman escrow transactions between users.
- To generate responses using the Google Gemini API (message content is sent to Google's API strictly for processing the current conversation and is not used to train our own models).

## Data Retention & Deletion
- Economy and RPG data are kept as long as you participate in the server.
- Chat memory used for AI context is limited to the last 2,000 messages globally and is constantly overwritten.
- You may request data deletion or an account reset by contacting a server administrator.

## Contact Us
If you have questions about your data, please contact the server owner or development team via the W2E Discord Server.
